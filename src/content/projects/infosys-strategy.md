> Below are some key learnings I gained while working on a change transformation and margin expansion program at Infosys. These insights come from hands on experience driving cross-functional initiatives, aligning stakeholders, and solving strategic business challenges.

## Key learnings

1. **Be Sharp & Quick For Executive Communication**
   Your mail should deliver clear insights + delegation cues that is readable and actionable under 2 mins or less
2. **Stakeholder Buy-In is Non-Negotiable to Drive Quality Results**
   - Not everyone voices disagreement, its your job to figure out if the alignment is genuine or a blanket yes
   - Test alignment → seek feedback → adapt → re-sell the vision
   - As a central team, always be humble and open to feedback - that's how strategy survives contact with reality.
3. **Influence Without Authority (Carrot > Stick)**
   For long-term collaboration, find levers beyond authority to get work done - clarity, alignment, purpose & recognition.
   Relationships often drive results more than authority
4. **Solve Structurally**
   Don't jump to conclusions. First, gather complete and unbiased data.
   Then: Understand → Diagnose → Ideate → Strategize→ Communicate → Execute
5. **Data ≠ Reality**
   Combine data with business context and on-ground nuances from teams to strategize
6. **Storytelling with Data**
   Analysis is just step one, narrative is what drives change.
   Use a simple structure: Setup → Conflict → Resolution
7. **Own Your Mistakes**
   If something goes wrong, Be transparent and take responsibility.
8. **Outcome > Output**
   Focus on real business impact and value addition, not just ticking boxes.
9. **Stay Rational, Not Reactive**
   Handle conflict and setbacks with logic, not emotion.

## Frameworks I follow

- **Pareto Principle** (80/20 Rule)
- **First Principles Thinking** (Break problems to their fundamentals and the reason upwards)
- **Inversion Technique** (Improve decisions by identifying what *not* to do.)
- **Parkinson's Law** (Set shorter deadlines to increase efficiency.)
- **2-Minute Rule** (Avoid procrastination on micro-tasks that take less than 2 minutes)
- **Inspiration is perishable** (When a new idea strikes, act on it immediately)

## My belief statements

- **Embrace simplicity in communication** - Make complex ideas clear and concise to minimize confusion and respect people's time
- **Work calmly, not anxiously** - Anxiety is energy wasted and clouds clarity.
- **Actively seek constructive feedback** - Growth thrives on honest input.
- **Listen more, speak less** - Understanding precedes meaningful contribution.
- **Smart work > Hard work** - Although, hard work builds character and perspective.
- **Consistency beats intensity**
- **Pragmatism in planning, optimism in execution**
- **Energy Management > Time Management**
- **Systems > Goals**
- **Quality > Quantity**
- **Learn → Reflect → Improve**
